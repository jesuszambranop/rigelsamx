Preparing your first M1 message

From data to print: what to check before getting started.

Define the information

Prepare the date, batch number or text you want to print. Check its accuracy before setting the message on the screen.

Check the available space

M1 print height is 12.7 mm. Review your message format and the usable surface of your packaging.

Test before production

Install the cartridge following the manual and make a handheld print on a sample. Check the code, legibility and adhesion.
