YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you get started.

Talk to an advisor
Does M1 use ink or laser?+

It uses thermal inkjet (TIJ) technology. In Mexico it is offered with the S70 solvent ink cartridge.

How does handheld printing work?+

Set the message on the screen and manually move the printer across the surface as directed in the manual. First test it on your material.

What can I print?+

Dates, batch numbers, text, logos, barcodes and QR codes. Review the size and format of your message with an advisor.

Which cartridge does it use?+

The compatible cartridge sold by Rigel Mexico is S70. You can request a replacement from its product page.

How do I know if it works on my material?+

Tell us the material, coating and surface conditions. Our team will advise you on ink evaluation and an adhesion test.
