S70 cartridge: planning consumption, care and replacement for your M1

The consumable is part of the coding process. A controlled template, production record and care routine help you order replacements in time and identify the cause of irregular printing.

Identify the consumable before installation

S70 is the solvent ink cartridge compatible with M1 offered by Rigel Mexico. When requesting a replacement, specify both models and share a photograph of the cartridge label. Keeping a record helps prevent reference mix-ups and supports application reviews with the sales or technical team.

Before installation, review the instructions supplied with the cartridge and printer. Handle the consumable carefully and do not force it into place. If you see damage, an unexpected label or a condition that differs from what you expect, contact Rigel before proceeding.

Why yield depends on the message

One cartridge does not produce the same unit count with every message. A short date and a multi-line message with a logo and code have different ink coverage. Trial prints, rejects, pauses and operating conditions also matter. A single print-count figure therefore cannot describe every process.

To estimate consumption, keep a representative template and record accepted units, trial prints and rejects for each period. Compare those records with actual replacements. When the design changes, compare new results with previous ones and update the forecast; do not automatically transfer yield assumptions between pack formats.

Create a routine for starting, pausing and finishing

At startup, confirm that the template matches the product and print a sample before continuing. Check that the message is complete and matches the approved placement. A brief inspection then helps prevent discovering a defect after many units have been processed.

During pauses and at shutdown, follow S70-specific protection and storage instructions. Do not borrow storage temperatures, idle-time limits or cleaning methods from a different cartridge. Protect the consumable from impact and contamination, and keep its instructions at the workstation.

An irregular print does not always mean low ink

If you see incomplete lines, distortion or ink transfer, identify the symptom before deciding on replacement. Check whether it occurs on every sample, in one part of the package or only after a handling change. Also check for changes to the substrate or coating.

Compare a recent print with an approved sample and record what changed. Avoid rubbing the cartridge print area with materials or liquids that the manufacturer has not specified. If the cause is unclear, stop adjusting and contact support: a focused photograph and structured description are more useful than multiple undocumented changes.

Set a reorder point using your own records

Plan replacement using observed consumption and the supply lead time confirmed by Rigel. One approach is to cover expected consumption during that lead time, with a reserve your company sets for demand variability. Keep measured data separate from estimates.

Update the forecast when production grows, a pack format is added or message coverage changes. Confirm availability and lead time when ordering rather than planning around an assumed delivery. Identify stored consumables and rotate them according to their instructions.

Prepare an inquiry that leads to action

For a replacement, specify M1, S70, required quantity and delivery city. For technical support, add photographs of the cartridge and print, the substrate, message and when the issue began. Explain whether it occurs at startup, after a pause or continuously.

That context helps Rigel Mexico guide the next review and keep your process organized. The aim is to base replacements on actual consumption and each intervention on an identified cause, reducing uncertainty before your next coding shift.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a fixed number of prints per S70?+

Yield depends on content, coverage and operating conditions. Track your application to plan replacements using your own production data.

What should I send if part of the message is missing?+

Send a print photograph, substrate details, template, cartridge model and when the symptom occurs. Avoid unspecified cleaning methods.
