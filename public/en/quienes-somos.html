About us

We are an international company supplying marking and coding equipment.

We offer solutions for small, medium and large businesses that need to identify their products and packaging with dates, batch numbers, text, logos and codes.

Founded in Ecuador, Rigel has developed a presence in Mexico, Colombia, Brazil, Venezuela and Chile. This international presence connects our coding experience with the commercial needs of each market.
