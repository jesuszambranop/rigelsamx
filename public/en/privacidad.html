Contact and careers forms

Rigel Mexico receives the information you provide to answer sales or technical inquiries. The careers form sends your profile and CV to Human Resources.

Contact inquiries go to ventasmexico@rigelsa.com. Applications go to rrhh@rigelsa.com. Do not include identity documents, financial data or unnecessary sensitive information.

Attachments

Résumés are emailed as PDF attachments. The website does not maintain a public document library or store résumés permanently in the site directory. Received emails are managed internally by Rigel.

Preferences and security

Your manual country choice may be stored in a cookie for one year. The PHP form uses a session cookie to protect submissions. The server limits attempts using a hashed IP reference for short periods. You can delete these cookies in your browser.

External services

WhatsApp, GitHub Pages, Google Maps and regional sites have their own policies. This website does not integrate advertising or analytics tools. The hosting provider may process technical logs needed to provide the service.

Access, correction or deletion

To ask about your data or request access, correction or deletion, email ventasmexico@rigelsa.com. Rigel will review your request through its inquiry procedure.
